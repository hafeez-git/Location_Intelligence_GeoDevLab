# Project Brief: Location Intelligence for Kaduna Metropolis

## 1. The Question

How can spatial data be used to understand the accessibility, environmental vulnerability, population context, healthcare availability, and settlement characteristics of locations in Kaduna Metropolis?

## 2. The Data I Need

- **OpenStreetMap (OSM)** — roads, schools, markets, supermarkets, parks, water bodies and other amenities. Source: https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html
- **GRID3 Nigeria Health Facilities v3.0** — locations and attributes of healthcare facilities. Source: https://data.grid3.org/datasets
- **WorldPop Nigeria Population v3.0** — gridded population estimates. Source: https://data.worldpop.org/repo/wopr/NGA/population/v3.0/
- **Copernicus DEM GLO-30/GLO-90** — elevation data for terrain and flood-vulnerability analysis. Source: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/
- **CHIRPS v3** — rainfall data for flood-vulnerability analysis. Source: https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS/v3.0/
- **GRID3 Nigeria Settlement Extents v4.1** — settlement extent and settlement context. Source: https://data.grid3.org/datasets/

## 3. The Analysis

The application will allow a user to enter a coordinate or select/draw an area within Kaduna Metropolis. It will use spatial analysis to identify nearby amenities and healthcare facilities, assess road accessibility, estimate population density, determine settlement context, and produce an indicative flood-vulnerability assessment using elevation, slope, rainfall and proximity to relevant water features. The results will be presented as location-specific indicators and interactive map layers.

## 4. What I Would Build

I will build an interactive web-based Location Intelligence application that allows users to select a location in Kaduna Metropolis and explore its accessibility, environmental vulnerability, population, healthcare and settlement context.
